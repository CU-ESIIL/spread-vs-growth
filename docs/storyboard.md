# ESA 2026 Fire Metabolism Storyboard

Source: `ESA 2026 Fire Metabolism.pdf`, a 12-slide Keynote export created on July 16, 2026.

## Talk Arc

1. **When wildfire becomes metabolic.** Introduce dimensional alignment and the rise of extreme wildfire.
2. **Is it spread or is it growth?** Define spread as motion and growth as metabolism.
3. **Wildfires do not follow diffusion.** Contrast predicted diffusion-like behavior with observed organized growth.
4. **Growth changes the rules.** Larger systems cannot simply become larger; geometry changes as systems scale.
5. **Growth discovers form.** Across nature, growth creates shape through constraint.
6. **Growth folds boundaries.** Boundary folding increases exchange.
7. **Growth needs more exchange.** Diffusion becomes limiting and transport networks organize.
8. **Biology solved this problem.** Biological transport systems improve exchange under scaling constraints.
9. **Life and fire share the same solution.** Life builds capture; fire builds release.
10. **This changes how we predict wildfire.** Geometry reveals rules and growth stage can determine the future.
11. **A new theory of wildfire.** Fire is fire in reverse: a transport system exporting energy through a dynamic boundary.
12. **Fire is life in reverse.** Synthesize the analogy and prediction framework.

## Animation Targets

- Animate the spread-vs-growth distinction as motion through space versus metabolic expansion.
- Show diffusion-like predictions diverging from observed organized wildfire growth.
- Morph simple shapes into folded boundaries to show why exchange surface matters.
- Build transport networks progressively from local exchange limits.
- Connect biological allometry to fire perimeter geometry using matched visual grammar.
- End with a synthesis animation that maps life processes to fire processes in reverse.
