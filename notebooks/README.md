# Notebooks

The numbered `01_...` through `09_...` notebooks are executable views over the tested `fire_metabolism` package. They cover the dimensional ladder, geometric constructions, boundary kinematics, growth laws, the conditional geometry-to-power bridge, counterexamples, all seven hypothetical worked examples, staged spatial-model experiment design, and the full first-principles derivation of the Figure 4 fire life cycle.

They intentionally import package functions instead of reimplementing equations in notebook cells. Passing a notebook calculation verifies a mathematical identity or consequence of its stated assumptions; it does not validate the empirical two-thirds or metabolic hypotheses.

Use this directory for exploratory figure development and storyboard experiments.

When a notebook becomes part of the repeatable workflow, move the stable logic into `scripts/` or `src/spread_vs_growth/`.
