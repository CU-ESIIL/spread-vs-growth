# Prediction And Falsification Hierarchy

The hierarchy runs from weak pattern agreement to tests that constrain the
proposed mechanism. A higher level does not repair failure at a lower required
measurement or closure level.

The realization-aware hierarchy now separates geometric scaling, endogenous
growth, realized growth, spatial reorganization, statistical detection of
constraint-like deficits, and causal attribution. FIRED reaches the fifth level
only probabilistically and generally cannot reach the sixth without independent
data. A realized forecast must state whether it assumes `B=1`, known `B`, or
only `0<=B<=1`; the last gives bounds rather than a unique trajectory.

## Minimal independent prediction set

The smallest set that covers distinct mathematical content is:

1. **Geometry:** the prespecified perimeter-area closure.
2. **Kinematics:** independent active-boundary motion closes area recruitment.
3. **Active-state geometry:** `Pa/A^(2/3)=kC`.
4. **Closed forcing:** normalized growth follows `beta0G(C,F)`.
5. **Coherence dynamics:** `C'/C=alpha(1-C)F-mu`.
6. **Energetic bridge:** independently measured chemical power follows the
   declared consumption closure.

The cube-root trajectory, time exponents, acceleration transition, connectivity
threshold, and asymptotic extinction are dependent consequences of subsets of
these closures. They are valuable tests, but they must not be counted as
independent evidential replications.

## Operational falsification ladder

| Level | Test | Meaning of failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Stable event, area, perimeter, resolution, and active-line definitions | The observation operator is not stable enough to test the theory. |
| 1 | Fixed-rule `P~A^(2/3)` | The proposed geometric closure fails; kinematics and broader energy accounting may still survive. |
| 2 | Independent boundary-motion accounting | The selected perimeter is not the recruiting boundary, untracked recruitment matters, or timing/velocity estimates are wrong. |
| 3 | `Pa/A^(2/3)=kC` | The coherence state does not control active boundary as proposed, or `k` is not stable. |
| 4 | Normalized forcing surface `A'/A^(2/3)=beta0G(C,F)` | The matching/velocity/fuel closure or constant `beta0` fails; an unconstrained effective coefficient is not a rescue. |
| 5 | Coherence dynamics and nullcline | The mass-action recruitment-loss equation is missing state or uses the wrong coherence quantity. |
| 6 | Prospective `sign(M')=sign(Psi)` and transition timing | The integrated two-state mechanism fails at its strongest dependent prediction, assuming lower-level measurements and closures passed. |
| Separate energetic branch | Independently measured chemical power versus recruited-area energy | The area-growth model may remain useful, but calling it metabolic is unsupported. |

Approximate cube-root linearity is a dependent diagnostic between Levels 1 and
4, not a separate rung: many mechanisms can produce locally stable transformed
growth.

## What would increase confidence in the mechanism?

Confidence increases most when independently measured `C` and prospective `F`
simultaneously satisfy the normalized forcing surface and coherence equation,
then correctly predict the sign of future `M'` on fires excluded from
calibration. A fitted `2/3` slope alone contributes little mechanism-specific
evidence because numerous alternatives reproduce it.
