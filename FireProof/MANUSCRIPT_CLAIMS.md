# Manuscript-Ready Claim Set

## Safe Main-Text Claims

1. “We propose a conditional two-state model in which cumulative burned area
   and a latent coherence state evolve jointly; the model is a falsifiable
   closure, not a derivation from combustion first principles.”

2. “If a common characteristic size gives compact area scaling and a
   four-thirds perimeter scaling, then perimeter scales as area to the
   two-thirds power; observing that slope does not uniquely identify either
   geometry or mechanism.”

3. “Under the specified matching closure, normalized area growth satisfies
   `A'/A^(2/3)=beta0[2CF/(C+F)]^2`, which is symmetric in coherence and
   remaining fuel and is quadratically limited by the smaller state.”

4. “The model’s ‘life cycle’ is a state-dependent transition: growth rate
   accelerates, is stationary, or decelerates according to the sign of an
   explicit balance equation. The equations do not require every fire to
   accelerate initially or to possess an interior or unique peak.”

5. “The strongest empirical test is prospective: independently estimate active
   boundary, reachable fuel, and coherence, fit parameters on development
   fires, and test whether the closed forcing and transition balance predict
   held-out growth-rate changes.”

6. “Area and perimeter trajectories alone do not generally identify the latent
   states and parameters; active-boundary and prospectively defined fuel
   observations are required.”

## Safe SI Claims

1. “For positive `C` and `F`, the product `CFeta(C/F)` is exactly
   `[2CF/(C+F)]^2`.”

2. “Although `eta` is maximized at `C=F`, the complete forcing is monotone in
   each positive state and saturates as the other state becomes abundant.”

3. “For `C<<F`, the closed forcing is asymptotic to `4C^2`; for `F<<C`, it is
   asymptotic to `4F^2`.”

4. “The metabolic stationary-point condition is the transition surface
   `Psi(A,C)=0`; acceleration and deceleration correspond to positive and
   negative sides of this surface.”

5. “A local metabolic maximum requires a positive-to-negative crossing of
   `Psi`, not merely a solution of `Psi=0`.”

6. “After scaling area by `Amax` and time by `1/alpha`, the model is controlled
   by `gamma=beta0/[alpha Amax^(1/3)]`, `delta=mu/alpha`, the initial area
   fraction `x0`, and the initial coherence `C0`.”

7. “Connectivity increases exactly when `(1-C)F>delta`; for `delta>=1`, it
   cannot increase anywhere in the biological unit square.”

8. “With positive initial states and the unchanged smooth closure, exact
   extinction is asymptotic rather than finite-time; observed abrupt endings
   require a measurement threshold or an omitted process.”

9. “The exponent statements `P~A^(2/3)`, `A~t^3`, and `P~t^2` are not three
   independent predictions because their exponents obey `2=(2/3)3`.”

10. “Cube-root area is affine only when the composite forcing `beta0CFeta` is
    constant; it is not a general lifecycle prediction.”

## Testable Predictions

1. “After independent construction of `C` and `F`, the quantity
   `A'/A^(2/3)` should collapse onto `[2CF/(C+F)]^2` with a stable calibrated
   coefficient over the claimed regime.”

2. “The observed sign of `C'` should agree prospectively with the sign of
   `(1-C)F-mu/alpha`.”

3. “The closed transition balance should predict the next-period sign of
   metabolic acceleration and the crossing into decline on held-out fires.”

4. “An independently observed active perimeter should satisfy
   `Pa/A^(2/3)=kC` more closely than total cumulative mapped perimeter.”

5. “Abrupt physical termination above the observation floor should coincide
   with an exogenous forcing, barrier, discontinuity, or intervention absent
   from the smooth closed model.”

## Claims Requiring Empirical Validation

1. “A stable two-thirds perimeter-area regime occurs in real wildfire under a
   prespecified observation rule and scaling range.”

2. “The latent coherence state corresponds to an independently measurable
   property of active connected fuel.”

3. “The prospectively delineated `Amax` represents reachable connected fuel at
   the forecast origin.”

4. “The active-boundary closure `Pa=kCA^(2/3)` and velocity closure
   `v_eff=v0Feta` remain stable across the claimed regime.”

5. “The reduced two-state model improves held-out prediction beyond recent
   area growth, weather, fuel, suppression information, and conventional spread
   models.”

6. “Area-growth rate is proportional to independently measured chemical power
   after accounting for fuel loading, residence time, and residual combustion.”

## Claims We Should Not Make

1. “A two-thirds perimeter-area slope proves wildfire is metabolic.”

2. “A two-thirds slope proves a fixed four-thirds fractal boundary.”

3. “The relations `P~A^(2/3)`, `A~t^3`, and `P~t^2` are three independent
   confirmations of the theory.”

4. “The model guarantees that every fire accelerates, reaches one interior
   metabolic peak, and then declines.”

5. “The matching optimum at `C=F` maximizes total growth forcing or shows that
   wildfire optimizes transport.”

6. “The continuous model reaches exact extinction at a finite death time.”

7. “Final realized burned area can be used as prospective `Amax`.”

8. “Cumulative mapped perimeter is active perimeter.”

9. “Area and mapped perimeter uniquely identify `beta0`, `C`, `F`, or `eta`.”

10. “Correct boundary signs alone prove interval invariance or that area cannot
    cross `Amax`.”

11. “A retrospective fit, oracle regime label, or future-defined fuel capacity
    is a forecast.”

12. “Failure of the two-thirds closure disproves all possible relationships
    between wildfire growth and energy processing.”
